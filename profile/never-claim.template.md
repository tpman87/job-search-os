# Never Claim — the boundaries you hold even when a JD wants otherwise
<!-- Copy to never-claim.md (gitignored). The writing skills check every draft against this. -->

The instinct under job-search pressure is to round yourself up toward what a job
description wants. This file exists to write down, in a calm moment, exactly where you
will not do that — so the boundary holds later when the pressure is real.

## Skills or tools you do not have working proficiency in
List things you might be tempted to claim because a JD asks for them and you're
"probably fine," or because you've used a tool a handful of times. **Plausibility is not
sourcing.** If a role genuinely needs something on this list, that's real information about
fit — surface it, don't paper over it.
- UNKNOWN

## Scope you do not actually have
Things you've been near but didn't own — a budget you influenced but didn't control, a
team you worked adjacent to but didn't lead, a decision you were consulted on but didn't
make. Precise language matters here: "advised on" is not "owned."
- UNKNOWN

## Numbers or outcomes you cannot independently verify
If a result was a team effort and you can't cleanly attribute your share of it, or a metric
moved for reasons partly outside your control, name that here so a resume draft doesn't
quietly claim it as solely yours.
