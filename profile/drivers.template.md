This is the single question that does the most work in this whole system, and it is the
one people answer worst on a first pass — because the honest answer is often not the
socially expected one (bigger title, bigger team, more prestige).

**Force a strict ranking. No ties.** If two things feel equally important, that's a sign
you haven't actually decided — sit with it rather than listing them as co-equal, because a
tie gives the evaluator nothing to break a close call with.

## Prompts, if the ranking above is hard to produce
Answer these in your own words first, then distill them into the ranked list above.

- If pay and role prestige conflicted directly — a lower-titled role that paid more and
  interested you more, versus a bigger title that paid less and interested you less — which
  do you actually pick? (Most people's honest answer surprises them here.)
- Do you want a big team, a small team, or does team size not move the needle for you at
  all? Be specific about *why* — "prestige of managing headcount" and "I like being
  hands-on" point to opposite-shaped roles.
- What's the difference between a role you'd tolerate and a role you'd be genuinely excited
  to do the actual day-to-day work of?
- Is there a *type* of work (not industry — work *shape*) you gravitate toward? Building
  new things vs. running mature systems. Customer-facing vs. internal-enablement. Individual
  craft vs. org-building. Name it plainly; don't round it up to "leadership."
- What's the one thing that, if a role lacked it, would make you decline even a great offer?
